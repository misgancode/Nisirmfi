About Nisir

Finance for the missing middle

Nisir serves micro, small and medium enterprises that banks overlook, along with low-income but economically active people and salaried employees. Because money doesn't come with instructions, we explain every product in plain words.

We live our promises to customers.

We are members of one Nisir family.

We continuously learn, evolve and adapt.

We finance sustainable progress through responsible finance.

D
Dawit WakgariChief Executive Officer

Vision

To be a beacon of swift and seamless financial solutions for underserved individuals and SMEs.

Mission

To provide trusted, win-win and need-based financial solutions to customers, and sustainable value to stakeholders, through a people-centered and intelligent system.
